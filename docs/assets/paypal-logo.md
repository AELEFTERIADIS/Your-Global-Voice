# PayPal payment-choice logo

`public/paypal-logo.svg` is the unchanged PayPal wordmark from PayPal's official
`paypal-sdk-logos` repository, `cdn/2.3.7/paypal-default.svg`, retrieved at commit
`4c39c1ec50b15dde3af99b524fb24ec8aa9fa11b`.

Source: https://github.com/paypal/paypal-sdk-logos/blob/4c39c1ec50b15dde3af99b524fb24ec8aa9fa11b/cdn/2.3.7/paypal-default.svg

The mark identifies the existing organization-authorized PayPal checkout link,
not an endorsement of Your Global Voice by PayPal.

No third-party image request is needed at runtime. The standard card-network logo row and its verification limits are documented
in `payment-brands.md`.
