`StripePaymentSheet/StripePaymentSheet/Resources/StripePaymentSheet.xcassets/Link/link_logo.imageset/Light.svg`

The logo retains its original colors and 72:24 proportions. Stripe's MIT license
is included in `link-logo-LICENSE.txt`. The mark identifies the existing payment
option; it does not imply endorsement of Your Global Voice by Stripe.

The organization reports seeing Link in its actual connected Stripe checkout.
American Express and PayPal are also confirmed by the organization's reports.
Visa, Mastercard and Discover still require account-specific acceptance evidence;
Apple Pay and Google Pay have not been confirmed and are not displayed. No
payment settings, checkout URLs, or credentials were changed.
