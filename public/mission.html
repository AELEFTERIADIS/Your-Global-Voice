Your Global Voice, Inc.

Our Mission

Our mission is to give a voice to the voiceless, bring hope and assistance to those in need, protect vulnerable lives, and inspire collective action toward a more compassionate and just world.

Our guiding direction

A voice for care.
A purpose for action.

Your Global Voice, Inc. is a nonprofit charitable organization based in New York, United States, with a commitment to serving both nationally and internationally. We believe that compassion knows no boundaries and that every act of kindness can make a difference. Guided by these principles, we seek to help individuals and communities, protect animals, and preserve the environment through meaningful action and charitable initiatives.

Read MoreRead Less

Humanitarian assistance is at the heart of our mission. We strive to help individuals and families facing poverty, hardship, illness, displacement, and other difficult circumstances. Through charitable projects, emergency assistance, and partnerships, we aim to provide essential support, relieve suffering, and bring hope to those who need it most. We believe that assistance should be guided by human need, without discrimination based on religion, race, nationality, gender, or personal background.

We are equally committed to assisting individuals who face persecution, discrimination, and injustice because of their religion, race, ethnicity, nationality, beliefs, or identity. We seek to defend human dignity, promote fundamental rights, and support those whose voices have been silenced. Through advocacy, awareness, and practical assistance, we aim to stand beside vulnerable individuals and help them live with dignity, safety, and freedom.

The protection of animals is a vital part of our mission. We are determined to help animals suffering from abuse, abandonment, neglect, and cruelty. Through rescue initiatives, veterinary assistance, shelter projects, and responsible adoption programs, we seek to save lives and provide animals with the care and protection they deserve. We also aim to promote humane treatment and encourage greater responsibility toward all living creatures.

Protecting our planet is a responsibility we share with present and future generations. We are committed to preserving forests, protecting natural habitats, and supporting efforts to prevent environmental destruction. Through conservation projects, education, and community participation, we seek to protect our natural resources and contribute to a healthier, safer, and more sustainable world.

Our mission extends beyond words and promises. We aspire to turn compassion into action through meaningful projects, dedicated volunteers, generous supporters, and partnerships built on shared values. We believe that lasting change is possible when individuals unite to help those in need, protect the vulnerable, and care for the world we share. Together, we can save lives, restore hope, and build a better future for all.

Our Four Purposes

The official organizational purposes from our Certificate of Incorporation.

  1. 01

    Religious, Racial, and Ethnic Persecution and Discrimination

    To provide charitable assistance, support, and advocacy for individuals and communities who face persecution or discrimination based on religion, race, ethnicity, nationality, or national origin, and to help address the consequences of such persecution or discrimination.

  2. 02

    Humanitarian Assistance and Relief

    To provide charitable humanitarian assistance and relief to individuals and communities in need through the provision of food, clothing, housing assistance, essential supplies, and emergency assistance.

  3. 03

    Animal Welfare Assistance

    To provide charitable financial assistance and support for the care and well-being of animals.

  4. 04

    Environmental Conservation and Sustainability

    To conduct and support charitable environmental conservation and sustainability initiatives, including the preservation and restoration of forests, the planting of trees, the protection of natural resources, and environmental improvement initiatives.
