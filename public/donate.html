Other Ways to Support Our Mission

We welcome inquiries regarding food, clothing, essential supplies, equipment, and other useful goods supporting our humanitarian, animal welfare, and environmental initiatives.

Corporate & Institutional Giving

Businesses, foundations, institutions, and philanthropic partners are welcome to inquire about financial and in-kind contributions supporting our initiatives.

For verified giving instructions or to discuss a contribution, email support@ygvoice.org.
