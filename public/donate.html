Planned support options

Many ways to make a difference

The following options are being presented for future planning. Availability and acceptance requirements will be confirmed before collection begins.

Financial support

Online financial donations

Credit and debit card donations, PayPal, and Zelle are potential future options. Bank transfers may also be considered. No accounts or payment links have been configured here. Use only verified instructions published by the organization once donations are enabled.

By mail

Checks

Check donations will require an approved payee name, a verified mailing address, and handling instructions. These details have not been published. Please do not send checks until they are confirmed.

In-kind contributions

Goods and essential supplies

Food, hygiene items, household essentials, and other appropriate goods may support future approved initiatives. Accepted items, condition requirements, quantities, and collection arrangements must be confirmed before any donation is sent.

Practical assistance

Clothing

Future clothing collections will need clear guidance on sizes, seasonal needs, item condition, and delivery arrangements. Please wait for an approved list of needed items and a verified collection location.

Across borders

International support

International financial or in-kind support will require confirmed acceptance arrangements. Currency, transfer fees, shipping costs, customs requirements, import restrictions, and local needs must be considered before a contribution is made.

Animals & environment

Purpose-aligned contributions

Support for animal welfare and environmental conservation will be guided by approved initiatives and their specific needs. No supplies, equipment, or designated funds are currently being solicited.

Before you contribute

  • Wait for verified donation instructions and a confirmed contact channel.
  • Confirm that your intended contribution can be accepted and is needed.
  • Arrange delivery or shipping only after receiving approved guidance; do not send unsolicited goods.
  • Do not send cash, bank details, or payment card information through unverified channels.
  • Tax treatment and receipt arrangements have not been confirmed. No tax-deductibility claim is made.

Receipt procedures and any designated-giving options will need organizational approval before donations begin. No such arrangements are currently offered.
